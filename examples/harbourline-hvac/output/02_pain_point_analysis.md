# Pain Point Analysis

Ranked by business value. Every pain point links to evidence in the input.

| ID | Pain point | Evidence | Frequency | At stake (assumption) | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Missed calls at peak times | "People calling about a no-heat don't leave voicemails, they call the next company." ~40 missed calls in a busy week | Daily in season | Lost jobs; an install is $5k to $12k. Share of new customers unknown | Medium |
| P2 | "Where's my tech" status calls | "Ten, fifteen calls a day in the busy season... the same loop every time." | 10 to 15 per day | ~4 office hrs/week (12 calls × 4 min × 5 days) | High |
| P3 | Double entry from Jobber to QuickBooks | "The sync... broke and nobody fixed it. So everything gets typed twice." About an hour a day | Daily | ~5 office hrs/week plus month-end reconciliation | High |
| P4 | Maintenance plan renewals lapse | "We had a bunch of plans lapse last year because nobody reached out." Estimated ~30 plans lost | Monthly | ~$6,000/yr (30 × ~$200), plus future install work | Medium |
| P5 | Slow quotes with no follow-up | "Some quotes take two or three days to go out. We don't really follow up." | Every install quote | Lower close rate on high-value installs (not quantified) | Medium |
| P6 | Manual Monday report | "Takes him most of the morning... quicker, not fancier." | Weekly | ~3 hrs/week of operations manager time | High |

What works well: a loyal maintenance-plan base, a scheduling system the team already knows, and a clear owner view of priorities.
