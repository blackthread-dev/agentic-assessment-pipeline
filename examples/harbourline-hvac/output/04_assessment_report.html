Executive Summary
Pain
Missed calls at peak times, repeated "where's my technician" calls, and invoices typed twice are consuming office time and letting urgent callers slip away, in a business with a loyal maintenance-plan base and a scheduling system the team already knows.
Outcome
Turning on features of the existing stack and repairing one broken integration can return about 6 office hours per week and stop maintenance-plan renewals from lapsing, without a platform migration.

Impact-Effort Matrix

Recommended Solutions
1
Top 3 priority

Automatic "On My Way" and Visit Reminder Texts

Recommended Tool: Scheduling tool notifications (existing)
Why This Fits: Customers get the update before they think to call. The office stops relaying messages between customers and technicians who are mid-job.
Complexity: plug-and-play
Monthly Cost: $0 if included in current plan
Setup Time: 1 to 2 hours
Time Saved: About 2 office hours per week
Addresses: P2
Cost source: needs verification
2
Top 3 priority

Repair the Scheduling-to-Accounting Sync

Recommended Tool: Existing Jobber to QuickBooks Online integration
Why This Fits: Invoices flow to accounting automatically, which removes the daily re-entry and the month-end mismatches it causes.
Complexity: some-setup
Monthly Cost: $0 (existing integration)
Setup Time: Half a day, including one week of reconciliation checks
Time Saved: About 4 office hours per week
Addresses: P3
Cost source: needs verification
3
Top 3 priority

Missed-Call Text-Back

Recommended Tool: Auto-reply feature from the business phone provider
Why This Fits: Every unanswered caller gets an instant text with a booking link, so urgent callers have a reason to wait instead of calling a competitor.
Complexity: plug-and-play
Monthly Cost: Varies by provider
Setup Time: 2 to 3 hours
Time Saved: Recovers callers who would otherwise hang up
Addresses: P1
Cost source: needs verification
4

Maintenance Plans as Recurring Jobs

Recommended Tool: Recurring jobs and automated reminders in the scheduling tool
Why This Fits: Moving plans out of the spreadsheet means tune-ups and renewals are scheduled and reminded automatically, even in the busiest weeks.
Complexity: project
Monthly Cost: $0 if included in current plan
Setup Time: 2 to 3 days to migrate about 350 plans
Time Saved: Protects about $6,000 a year in plan revenue
Addresses: P4
Cost source: needs verification
5

Same-Day Quotes with Automatic Follow-Up

Recommended Tool: Quote templates on a tablet plus automatic quote follow-ups
Why This Fits: Quotes go out from the driveway instead of the kitchen table, and follow-ups happen without anyone remembering to send them.
Complexity: some-setup
Monthly Cost: $0 if included in current plan
Setup Time: 1 to 2 days to build templates
Time Saved: Quotes out the same day
Addresses: P5
Cost source: needs verification

Assumptions & Sources
  • Status calls: 12 per day at about 4 minutes each over 5 days is about 4 hours per week; automated texts are assumed to remove half.
  • Double entry: the owner's estimate of about 1 hour per day; a working sync is assumed to remove most of it.
  • Lapsed plans: the operations manager's estimate of about 30 plans at about $200 per year each.
  • Missed calls: the share of the roughly 40 weekly missed calls that were new customers is unknown, so no revenue is claimed for recommendation 3.
  • Feature availability depends on the current plan tier of each tool and has not been verified in this sample run.
