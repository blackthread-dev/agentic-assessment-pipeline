# Follow-Up Walkthrough Prep

## Agenda (15 minutes)
1. Confirm the six pain points match their reality (2 min).
2. Walk through the top 3 (5 min).
3. Ask which one matters most right now (2 min).
4. Agree on the 4-day plan start date (3 min).
5. Park the bigger projects for a later conversation (3 min).

## Three points to emphasize
- Nothing here requires replacing Jobber. It matches what Dana said: "we're just not using half of it."
- The sync repair is the fastest relief for the office (about 4 hours a week).
- Missed-call text-back is about the urgent no-heat caller, before the next cold snap.

## Likely questions
| Question | Plain answer |
|---|---|
| "Will customers find the texts annoying?" | They replace calls customers are already making to ask the same thing. Frequency can be limited to one reminder and one "on my way". |
| "What if the sync breaks again?" | The plan includes a week of reconciliation checks and a simple monthly check so it gets caught in days, not years. |
| "Should we just get an AI receptionist?" | Not first. Text-back is cheaper, lower risk on urgent calls, and shows how many callers are really being lost. |

## Strongest next step
Migrate maintenance plans into recurring jobs before the renewal cycle: it protects recurring revenue and the customers most likely to buy the next install.
