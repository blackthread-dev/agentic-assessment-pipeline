# Cleaned Notes

## 1. Business overview
- Residential heating and cooling: installs, repairs, maintenance.
- About 350 customers on maintenance plans.
- Strong seasonality; the first fall cold snap drives a surge in calls.

## 2. Team and roles
- 14 people: owner (Dana), operations manager (Sam), 2 office staff (including Kim), 9 technicians, 1 warehouse.
- Comfort advisor visits sites for install quotes (needs confirmation: dedicated role or an owner/tech duty).

## 3. Current tools
- Jobber: scheduling, quotes, job completion, payments.
- QuickBooks Online: accounting.
- Standard business phone line ringing the office.
- Excel: maintenance plan tracking, weekly report.

## 4. Customer / job flow
- Customer calls the office → office books in Jobber → tech completes job and adds notes and parts in Jobber → office creates invoice → invoice re-entered in QuickBooks.

## 5. Repetitive tasks
- Answering "when is my tech coming" calls, then relaying through the tech.
- Re-entering every invoice into QuickBooks.
- Monthly filtering of the maintenance spreadsheet and manual outreach.
- Weekly report built by copying Jobber data into Excel.

## 6. Bottlenecks and delays
- Calls go to voicemail when both office staff are busy; urgent callers do not leave messages.
- Install quotes take 2 to 3 days to send; no follow-up process.
- Renewal outreach stops in busy periods.

## 7. Manual reports and spreadsheets
- Maintenance plan spreadsheet (owned by Sam).
- Monday performance report, most of a morning to build.
