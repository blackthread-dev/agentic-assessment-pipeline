# Workflow Assessment: Harbourline Heating & Cooling

*Assessment date: September 12, 2026. Fictional example.*

## Impact at a glance

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Weekly office time returned | about 6 hours |
| Plan revenue protected yearly | about $6,000 |
| New monthly tool cost | limited to missed-call text-back (price needs verification) |

## Executive summary

**Pain.** Missed calls at peak times, repeated "where's my technician" calls, and invoices typed twice are consuming office time and letting urgent callers slip away, in a business with a loyal maintenance-plan base and a scheduling system the team already knows.

**Outcome.** Turning on features of the existing stack and repairing one broken integration can return **about 6 office hours per week** and stop maintenance-plan renewals from lapsing, without a platform migration.

**Top 3 priorities**
1. Automatic "on my way" and visit reminder texts (P2)
2. Repair the scheduling-to-accounting sync (P3)
3. Missed-call text-back (P1)

## Recommendations

| # | Recommendation | Addresses | Setup | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Automatic "on my way" and visit reminder texts (**Top 3**) | P2 | 1 to 2 hours | About 2 office hours per week |
| 2 | Repair the scheduling-to-accounting sync (**Top 3**) | P3 | Half a day plus a week of checks | About 4 office hours per week |
| 3 | Missed-call text-back (**Top 3**) | P1 | 2 to 3 hours | Recovers callers who would otherwise hang up |
| 4 | Maintenance plans as recurring jobs | P4 | 2 to 3 days | Protects about $6,000 a year |
| 5 | Same-day quotes with automatic follow-up | P5 | 1 to 2 days | Quotes out the same day |

## 4-day quick-win plan

1. Turn on visit reminders and "on my way" texts.
2. Set up missed-call text-back with a booking link.
3. Repair the accounting sync and start reconciliation checks.
4. Build install quote templates and turn on quote follow-ups.

## After the quick wins

1. Migrate maintenance plans into recurring jobs before the next renewal cycle.
2. Replace the Monday spreadsheet with a saved report.
3. Review after one busy-season month: missed calls, status calls, sync errors.

## Assumptions

- Status calls: 12 per day at about 4 minutes each over 5 days is about 4 hours per week; texts assumed to remove half.
- Double entry: owner's estimate of about 1 hour per day; a working sync assumed to remove most of it.
- Lapsed plans: about 30 plans at about $200 per year each.
- Missed calls: new-customer share unknown, so no revenue is claimed for recommendation 3.
- Feature availability depends on each tool's plan tier and was not verified in this sample run.
